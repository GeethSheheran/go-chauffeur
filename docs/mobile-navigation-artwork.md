## Final generation prompt

Create a polished 3D PRODUCT-ICON atlas for a premium travel website's mobile navigation. The TWO attached images are the authoritative MATERIAL AND RENDERING REFERENCES: image 1's precisely manufactured smooth charcoal megaphone; image 2's finely modeled wood, clear glass and sand hourglass. Translate their sophisticated object-rendering language into the five navigation objects below. Do not draw megaphones or hourglasses. Do not use any earlier generated icon set as a style reference.

The critical style is CLEAN, PRECISE OBJECT MODELING WITH BELIEVABLE INDIVIDUAL MATERIALS and subtle studio illumination. Smooth manufactured surfaces, crisp curved edges with small accurate bevels, carefully joined parts, clear structural depth. Actual distinctions between satin paint, rubber, glass, ceramic, wood and metal. Fine wood grain only where wood occurs; NOT a uniform grain over everything. The quality of the supplied product renders is the target. No rough clay, no powder texture, no felt, no fuzzy micrograin, no plaster, no inflated cartoon shapes, no paper-craft terrain, no miniature landscape scenes, no low-poly appearance.

LAYOUT: exactly 1536x1024 landscape transparent PNG, 3 columns and 2 rows of equal 512-square cells, no visible grid. Five separate icons with generous empty transparent margin. Each main object fills roughly 72-78% of its cell, centered precisely at (256,256), (768,256), (1280,256), (256,768), (768,768). Bottom-right cell (1280,768) completely EMPTY transparent. All parts and very faint shadows stay inside their cells.

Top-left HOME: a single carefully modeled clean house-shaped object, warm ivory ceramic walls, warm terracotta satin-finish pitched roof, forest-green inset front door, two simple glass windows. Precise architectural edges with soft bevels; a restrained premium object. No garden, no terrain, no pedestal, no shrubs, no diorama.
Top-middle PLACES: one sculptural emerald-green location pin with a circular opening, smooth satin-enamel surface, a thin warm silver edge visible on its side. This is a clean well-made 3D physical object, not a balloon. No map, no terrain, no base.
Top-right PLAN: a clean refined green chauffeur sedan as a small premium die-cast model, natural sedan proportions, precisely formed body panels, light blue-grey glass windows, charcoal rubber tires, restrained brushed metal wheel centres and small ivory lights. Smooth satin green paint, controlled highlights like the megaphone reference, no mirror-polished car advertisement finish, no chrome glare. No base, no road, no people.
Bottom-left THINGS TO DO / EXPLORE: a handsome compact travel compass, circular warm natural oak outer ring like the hourglass's wood, fine charcoal inner housing, clear glass face, ivory dial, one forest-green needle and a small warm copper needle end. Minimal clear directional marks but NO letters or numbers. A precise sophisticated small object, not a cartoon. Tilted slightly toward the viewer so the compass reads.
Bottom-middle CHAT: two overlapping physically constructed speech bubble objects, front emerald-green smooth satin polymer, back warm ivory ceramic. Modest believable thickness, crisp rounded corners, precise small bevels, three inset ivory dots on front. No overly inflated gummy shapes. No logos.

CAMERA: consistent tasteful elevated three-quarter studio product view like the references, around 20-25 degrees above object level, medium telephoto/orthographic feeling, all objects facing coherently. Colour should be appealing and natural: fresh emerald green (#28A56D), warm ivory, terracotta, oak honey brown, light blue-grey glass, charcoal rubber and warm silver. Rich clean natural colours, not desaturated sage monochrome, not neon.

LIGHT: huge diffuse softbox and broad ambient fill, neutral high-key studio illumination like the supplied references. Smooth gentle tonal gradients define curved surfaces. Very controlled soft highlights needed to show material, never shiny glossy plastic glare. Almost no ground shadow: only a tiny feather-light neutral-grey contact cue, no long or dark shadow, no vignette, no glow, no halo. User specifically dislikes excessive shadows and shine.

BACKGROUND: genuine transparent alpha everywhere outside the objects. No white rectangles or per-cell background, no labels, no watermark, no typography, no logos. At mobile navigation scale these must read as precise clean rendered physical objects, not clay illustrations.
