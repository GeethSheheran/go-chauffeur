## Chauffeur tip portrait

Generated using the built-in image generation tool on 15 November 2026, with `chauffeur-stylized.webp` as the character reference. Saved at `public/images/illustrations/chauffeur-guide.webp`, 384 px wide with transparency. Prompt: “Create a small website avatar, a close cropped head-and-shoulders portrait of ONLY the friendly stylized chauffeur in the reference. Preserve exactly his simple rounded clay-like 3D character identity: warm medium tan skin, swept charcoal hair, small black oval eyes, small smiling mouth, cream shirt and dark forest-green tie. Front-facing with slight three-quarter angle, head and upper chest fills a square frame and is clearly readable at 48px. Soft matte materials, smooth simple surfaces, bright natural colors, very gentle light, no shine, no harsh or cast shadows. Transparent background. No car, no steering wheel, no props, no circle or border, no text, no realistic human features. This is a matching character portrait for chauffeur advice boxes, not a new character.”

The portrait was revised after client feedback to a lighter warm golden beige skin tone using the built-in image generation tool. Revision prompt: “Edit ONLY this existing chauffeur avatar’s skin tone. Make his face, ears and neck noticeably lighter: a soft warm light-medium golden beige complexion, approximately two shades lighter, natural and warm, not pale white and not orange. Keep exactly the same friendly stylized 3D character identity, charcoal swept hairstyle, tiny oval dark eyes, smile, cream shirt, forest green tie, head shape, scale, pose, framing and matte clay-like materials. Soft even lighting and no gloss. Keep the transparent background. Keep the full head and upper chest visible with room at the bottom for a website CSS fade. Do not add any props, vehicle, text, borders, or background. All other appearance must be unchanged.” The displayed portrait fades into the tip panel at its lower edge. A single gentle greeting tilt runs when it enters the viewport; reduced-motion users receive a static portrait.
